# Deep Dives

Byte-level, mechanic-first treatments of individual vulnerability classes — the *why it works*
and the exact steps, not the checklist. Companion to the overview notes in
[`../methodology/`](../methodology/).

All content is for authorized testing and defense. Techniques here are publicly documented; the
point is understanding them well enough to find, exploit (on systems you may test), and fix them.
